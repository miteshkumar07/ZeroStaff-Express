The problemEnough buses, not enough drivers

Nuremberg wants fast express buses to its big job clusters, like Nordostpark. It has the vehicles but not the drivers, and hiring takes months and costs about €60,000 per driver per year. The usual answer, "hire more people", doesn't work.

Our question: can we create an express line using only the staff and buses that are already on the road?

An X30 round trip needs about 40 minutes, so there are no "free" buses

So instead of pretending there were spare buses, we asked a smarter question: which existing trips can be handed over to the express line, and what exactly does that cost?

Our solutionHand over a long loop, run an express, be back on time

Some Line 44 buses run a 71-minute loop (Hbf → Langwasser → Hbf). If that loop is replaced by an X30 round trip (Hbf → Nordostpark → Hbf, about 40 min), the same bus and driver are back at Hbf in time for their next normal trip, and the driver even gets extra rest.

1

Collect real operations

Real VAG departures, vehicle numbers and verified return trips are converted automatically into one input file.

2

List every option

Every possible X30 departure, and every Line 44 loop that could be handed over, with the bus back on time.

3

Optimize under hard rules

Zero new staff, normal service otherwise unchanged, ≤ 4 h of work without a 15-min break, ArbZG §4 break totals, a 10 h daily cap, and never two cancellations in a row on a branch.

4

Pick the best plan

The most express service for commuters first, then minimal disruption and empty driving. The solver proves it is optimal.

5

Verify independently

A separate checker re-tests every plan minute by minute: break laws, timetable, locations, one driver per trip.

6

Explore live

In the dashboard, change the rules (headway target, disruption budget) and re-solve in about 1–4 seconds.
