The problemEnough buses. Not enough drivers.

Nuremberg wants fast buses to its job hubs, like Nordostpark. Vehicles exist, but drivers don't, and hiring takes months and costs about €60,000 per driver per year. So we asked: can we launch an express with the staff already there?

Hard rules, always

Zero new staff, normal service otherwise unchanged, ≤ 4 h of work without a 15-min break, ArbZG §4 break totals, a 10 h daily cap, never two missing buses in a row.

Exact to the minute

Each driver's day is cut at every real start/end time, so breaks are modelled exactly. Doing nothing is always a valid plan, so the optimizer can't get stuck.

Also works with idle time

For depots with split shifts and standby drivers: a 40-driver test depot runs an express every 30 min, fully covered, with 0 hires.
